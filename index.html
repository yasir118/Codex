PROJECT NOTES

About this study

Contour House is a conceptual residence on an alpine ridge at 1,280 m. Three curved terraces are cut into the slope, each one's roof becoming the garden of the level above, with a glass lookout pavilion at the top.

  • Explode the house by system, or show and isolate each layer
  • Cut a long section through the main hall
  • Move the sun and follow the light through the central oculus
  • Click any part of the model to learn its role

The model is an abstract reconstruction for explaining ideas, not a measured survey.
